# Mavita Sport

### What does our product do?

This variant, which contains the main component silicon, ensures a faster recovery from injuries, when it is daily taken. It also prevents acidification of muscles and it takes care of a better endurance.

In short, Mavita Sport takes care of:

* Faster recovery from injuries;
* Prevention of acidification;
* Better endurance.

**Energized suspension of bioavailable silica and 8 selected MINERALS.**
