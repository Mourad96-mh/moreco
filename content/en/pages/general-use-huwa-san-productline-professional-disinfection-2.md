# Professional disinfection

![huwasan_beeldmerk](http://moreco.ma/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/huwasan_beeldmerk.png)Huwa-San is the new generation of biodegradable disinfection. The Huwa-San products are ecological disinfectants based on activated and stabilised hydrogen peroxide. The unique way of activating and stabilising is proven highly effective, f.i. in legionella control and removal of biofilm. Huwa-San is a safe, odourless, alcohol-free and eco-friendly disinfectant solution for applications in; Agriculture, Veterinary industry, Food industry and even for (drinking) water disinfecting.

### Huwa-San AGRO

#### Desinfection For agriculture

**A sustainable way to secure your growth**

Your perfect partner to keep your crops healthy and save them from bacteria. Huwa-San Agro is the new generation hydrogen peroxide which overcomes water quality issues while supporting robust plant health.

[More information](http://moreco.ma/general-use/huwa-san-productline/professional-disinfection-2/agriculture/)
[Download safetysheet](http://moreco.ma/public/huwa-san/product_safety_data_sheet_huwa_san_tr_-_50.pdf)

### Direction for use

**Intended users:**

Greenhouses, hydro culture, horticulture, floriculture, juvenile producers, seed growers, …  
 Huwa-San 50 AGRO is suitable to kill bacteria, fungi, viruses, spores and algae.

**Intended use:**

* Irrigation water disinfection
* Removal of biofilm in water systems (sprinkler system, drip irrigation, circulation water)
* Disinfection of surfaces, floors, walls and equipment in agriculture (greenhouses, hydroculture, hydroponics, …)
* Soil/substrate disinfection
* Surface disinfection of total greenhouses through vaporization of the product (“fogging”)

### Huwa-San VET

#### Disinfection voor veterinary applications

**Encouraging growth, reducing stress**

Huwa-San disinfection is a very safe and effective method for removing the risk of most parasites, bacteria, fungi and viruses and can be used for infection control, algae growth prevention and biofilm removal as well as for conditioning rinsing and washing waters. Using Huwa San to provide a sterile drinking-water supply to animals encourages growth, reduces stress and allows the development of healthy animals. Sterile water also allows vitamins and medicines to be delivered easily, and ensures that there is no possibility of ingress for pathogens and viruses that could increase mortality.

[More information](http://moreco.ma/general-use/huwa-san-productline/professional-disinfection-2/veterinary/)

### Direction for use

**Intended users:**

Cattle husbandries, poultry farms, dairy farms, horse breeding …  
 Huwa-San 50 VET is suitable for killing bacteria, fungi, viruses, spores.

**Intended use:**

* Disinfection of drinking water for animal use
* Removal of organic scaling (biofilm) in drinking water systems (storage tanks, pipe systems, nipples, cups,…)
* Disinfection of surfaces, floors, walls and equipment in animal husbandries such as cattle breeding, dairy, poultry, egg production
