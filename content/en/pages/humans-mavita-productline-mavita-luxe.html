Recipe for the Mavita luxury Smoothie

Want to learn how to make Grapefruit Carrot Ginger Mavita Smoothie? Grapefruit, ginger and carrots have marvelous health benefits. So why not try it?

As with all citrus fruit, remove the seeds and peel them. With grapefruit, you can leave as much of the pith on to get extra fiber and nutrients. Always discard the outer peel.

Ingredients:

  • 1 grapefruit
  • 4 medium carrots
  • Fresh ginger
  • 1 teaspoon Mavita Luxe powder

Instructions:

  • Squeeze the grapefruit, carrots and ginger together
  • Put the Mavita Luxe powder in a glass and pour the juice.
  • Stir and enjoy!
