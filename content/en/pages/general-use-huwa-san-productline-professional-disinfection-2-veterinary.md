# Veterinary

### Huwa-San VET

**25% – 50%**

Huwa-San Technology is very effective to treat animal drinking water and to control contamination from the pipe systems, even at very low concentrations. Huwa-San is eco-responsible. It breaks down 100% into water and oxygen. Huwa-San is user-friendly, safe to handle, easy to implement and simplifies the process. Decreases morbidity and mortality in farm animals. It improves the animal health and decreases use of antibiotics for optimizing water quality.

**Advantages:**

* Removes biofilm: eliminates and prevents blockages in the water pipes and nozzles.
* 100% biodegradable.
* Does not foam.
* Odourless and colourless.
* No toxic residue.
* Completely mixable with water.
* Active at temperatures up to 95°C and in a broad pH range.
* Remains active and stable in the absence of pollution (“depot effect”).
* No possibility of ingress for pathogens and virusses that could increase mortality.
* Enchances the quality of the animals’ living conditions, resulting in healthier, bigger animals and a lower mortality rate.
