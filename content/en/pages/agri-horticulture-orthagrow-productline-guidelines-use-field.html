Guidelines for use in the field

How should Orthagrow Control be used?

 That is very easy! Just 2 simple steps: 1. Mix with water and 2. Spray on the leaves

Check out the results of our research.

  1. Sow or transplant the crops as per the recommended spacing.
  2. Apply the recommended dose of fertilizer to the crop.
  3. Use only 2.0 liters of Orthagrow Control per hectare for each crop per harvest.
  4. Use Orthagrow Control in four sprays at 15-20 days interval according to crops. e.g. for tomato use at 15 days interval (15, 30, 45 & 60 days after transplanting) & for cotton use at 20 days interval (20,40, 60 & 80 days after sowing)
  5. Use 1.0 ml Orthagrow Control per liter of water for spraying.
  6. Use 500 liters of water per hectare for spraying.
  7. Mix Orthagrow Control in water to get the homogenous dilution and apply as foliar spray.
  8. Spraying preferably be done in early morning or evening.
  9. Avoid the periods of high wind and rainfall for spraying.
  10. Orthagrow Control can be mixed in all types of agro-chemicals,(i.e. pesticides, liquid fertilizers, etc.) but care should be taken to mix Orthagrow Control last to the solution just before application.
  11. Follow two hand weedings at 20 and 40 days after sowing or transplanting.
  12. Follow pesticide spraying schedule as and when necessary.
  13. Store Orthagrow Control in cool & dry place away from direct sunlight.

Stages of Spraying

Below you can see the various stages of spraying per crop. Have a look at the results of our research when applying Orthagrow Crontrol.

Vegetables
15, 30, 45 and 60 days after sowing / transplanting

Cotton
30, 60, 90 and 120 days after sowing

Pulses
15, 30,45 and 60 days after sowing

Oilseeds 
15, 30, 45 and 60 days after sowing / transplanting

Fruit
1st time before flowering & 4 sprays at 15 days interval after flowering

Cereals
20, 40, 60 and 80 days after sowing

Sugarcane
60, 90, 120 and 150 days after planting

Rice
20, 40, 60 and 80 days after transplanting

Flowers
Every 30 days interval
