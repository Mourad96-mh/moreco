# Food & Beverage

### Huwa-San Food & Beverage

**35% – 50%**

Huwa-San F&B products are ideal for the disinfection of surfaces, equipment and machinery in all kinds of food processing companies. Rinsing of treated surfaces is not required. Thanks to its efficiency at high temperatures, these Huwa-San F&B products are an excellent disinfectant for pipes, tubes and bottles in various production processes. Washing and spraying fruits and vegetables during processing or packaging with diluted Huwa-San F&B products increase freshness and sell-by date.

**Advantages:**

* Removes biofilm and prevents recontamination in the water supply.
* Re-use of processwater.
* Extremely efficient bactericide, fungicide and virucide.
* Bacteria can’t build up resistance against Huwa-San F&B.
* Leaves no toxic residue which might compromise the quality of the end product.
* Has no influence on the endproduct: odourless, colourless, tasteless.
* Improves freshness and sell-by date.
