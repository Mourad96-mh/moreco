# Water Treatment

### Huwa-San Water Treatment

**25% – 50%**

Huwa-San WATER products can be used to disinfect your entire water system, to remove biofilm and to prevent Legionella. Huwa-San Water is a biodegradable alternative to existing products on the basis of halogens, chlorine, bromine, iodine, aldehydes, quaternary ammonium compounds.

Huwa-San 50 WT is suitable to kill bacteria, fungi, viruses, spores, algae and amoebae.
